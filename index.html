Construction

Buildings cost money, materials and an idle astronaut. Construction takes simulated hours.

Maintenance & Structures

Astronauts not building or researching automatically repair the most worn units. Repairs consume materials, and every structure costs money to keep running.

Structural condition

Crew Health

Astronauts exposed to solar storms can fall ill. Move the crew into a shelter during a storm.

Research Tree

Click a research icon to start it. Each project occupies astronauts: the more you dedicate, the faster it finishes. A failed project loses every invested resource.

Earth Imports

Every kilogram launched from Earth is expensive. Imports include a flat launch fee. Bulk logistics research lowers the price.

Missions

One mission per year. Failing a mission ends the run; success brings supplies, funds, crew or research bonuses.
